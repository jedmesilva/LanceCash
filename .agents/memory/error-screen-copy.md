---
name: Error screen copy
description: Product requirements for the loading-failure screen.
---

The loading-failure screen should say only “Falha ao carregar esta tela” and “Tente novamente em instantes.” Keep the retry button. Do not mention instability, account verification, balance, bids, or protections.

**Why:** the user said these explanations create uncertainty and mistrust, and account or balance details are inappropriate before people have accounts.

**How to apply:** Keep this screen focused on the failed load and retry action; do not add technical, account, or financial reassurance.
